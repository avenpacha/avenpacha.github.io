Learning Analytics in Higher Education

Reviewing student-engagement research and communicating its implications and limitations for higher education.

For this group assignment, we reviewed a published research article introducing the Student Engagement Dataset (SED), which brings together online learning activity and academic-performance data.

Project Overview

Our group reviewed the article "Student Engagement Dataset (SED): An Online Learning Activity Dataset," published in IEEE Access in 2025.

The article presents a dataset derived from a university's Moodle-based learning environment. It brings together information about students' online activities and academic performance to support research in learning analytics.

Our presentation examined the study's purpose, data preparation methods, dataset structure, correlation analysis, limitations, and potential uses in higher education.

Project Scope

This was a review and presentation of published research. The dataset, original analysis, and research findings belong to the article's authors; our group did not conduct the original study.

Why Learning Analytics Matters

Learning management systems record information about how students interact with course materials, complete activities, and participate in online learning environments.

The article explores how these records can be organized into a dataset for studying patterns of student engagement and their relationship with academic performance.

This topic is particularly relevant to my interest in educational technology and how institutions can use data to better understand and support students.

Research Review

Our group divided the article into sections, with each member responsible for explaining a different part of the research.

Dataset Construction

Reviewed how the original researchers extracted, cleaned, anonymized, and organized learning activity and grade data.

Data Records & Analysis

Examined the structure of the student engagement dataset and the types of information it contains.

Correlation Analysis

Reviewed the article's analysis of relationships between student engagement indicators and academic performance.

I presented the final portion of our review, focusing on what researchers should consider when using the dataset and how its limitations affect the interpretation of findings.

My Role & Contributions

My assigned section of the group presentation covered the article's usage notes, limitations, and conclusion.

I reviewed these sections to explain both the dataset's potential value for learning analytics and the considerations that affect how its findings can be used.

Dataset Usage

Discussed how student activity records can reveal patterns of engagement beyond final course grades.

Research Limitations

Explained considerations such as skewed activity data, limited demographic information, and the study's single-semester scope.

Educational Implications

Summarized potential uses of learning analytics for understanding engagement, supporting students, and informing course design.

Technical Communication

Preparing and recording my section gave me an opportunity to communicate research findings to an audience, explain limitations, and connect the article's technical content to practical questions in higher education.

Interpreting the Research

One important part of my presentation was explaining why data should be interpreted in the context of how it was collected and what it represents.

The article draws on data from a specific university and academic semester, with limited demographic information. These characteristics should be considered before applying findings to other student populations or learning environments.

The group's presentation also addressed why statistical significance should not be confused with the practical strength of a relationship between student activity and academic performance.

Key Takeaway

Learning analytics can help institutions explore patterns in student activity, but the data and research limitations matter when interpreting results or considering practical applications.
